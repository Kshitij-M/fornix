# Code of Conduct

Fornix is a public project. We want technical disagreement to be rigorous,
curious, and useful, while keeping participation safe for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, discussions, and code;
- explaining technical reasoning and trade-offs without personal attacks;
- welcoming questions and correcting mistakes in good faith;
- focusing criticism on the work, proposal, or behavior rather than a person;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and the project’s license obligations.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing someone else’s private information without permission;
- threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, or targeted trolling;
- sharing credentials, private repository content, or exploit details in public
  project spaces;
- knowingly violating the project license or misrepresenting copied work.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, review
threads, discussions, documentation, events, and other project spaces. Project
maintainers may remove or edit material, close discussions, restrict
participation, or take other proportionate action when behavior violates this
policy.

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, report it privately
through the repository’s available maintainer or security-reporting channel.
Do not include sensitive personal information or security details in a public
issue. If no private channel is visible, open a minimal non-sensitive issue and
request private follow-up.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
